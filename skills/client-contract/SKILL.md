# Client Contract

You are an expert at drafting clear agency-client agreements that protect both parties and set the engagement up for success. Ambiguity in contracts causes disputes — every clause should leave no room for different interpretations.

**Important:** This is a starting point based on common agency practice — not legal advice. Recommend attorney review for engagements over $10,000 or involving sensitive IP or exclusivity.

## Before You Start

**Read client context first:**
Load `.agents/clients/[slug]/product-marketing.md` and confirm the proposal terms before drafting.

Confirm (ask if not in client files):
1. **Parties** — Agency legal name and client legal name
2. **Scope** — Services and deliverables (from proposal)
3. **Fees** — Monthly retainer or project fee, payment schedule
4. **Term** — Start date, minimum commitment, renewal terms
5. **IP** — Does the client own all deliverables, or does the agency retain any rights?
6. **Exclusivity** — Any restriction on the agency working with competitors?

## Contract Structure

### Parties and Effective Date

```
MARKETING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Marketing Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] 
("Effective Date") by and between:

Agency: [Agency Legal Name], a [State] [LLC/Corp] ("Agency"), and

Client: [Client Legal Name], a [State] [LLC/Corp/Individual] ("Client").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties."
```

---

### 1. Services

```
Agency agrees to perform the marketing services described in Exhibit A (Statement 
of Work) attached to and incorporated into this Agreement ("Services").

Any changes to the scope of Services must be agreed in writing via a Change Order 
signed by both parties before additional work begins. Work performed outside the 
agreed scope without a signed Change Order will be billed at Agency's standard 
hourly rate of $[RATE]/hour.
```

**Exhibit A — Statement of Work** (attach the proposal deliverables table here verbatim, with:)
- Service descriptions
- Deliverable list
- Estimated timeline
- Explicit out-of-scope items

---

### 2. Fees and Payment

**Retainer:**
```
Client agrees to pay Agency a monthly retainer of $[AMOUNT], billed on the 
[1st] of each month, payable within [15] days of invoice.

The retainer covers the Services described in Exhibit A. Hours or deliverables 
beyond the retainer scope will be billed separately per Change Order.

Ad spend, platform fees, stock assets, and third-party tool costs are not 
included in the retainer and will be invoiced separately or billed directly 
to Client's accounts.
```

**Project:**
```
Total project fee: $[AMOUNT]
Payment schedule:
  - 50% ($[X]) due upon signing this Agreement
  - 50% ($[X]) due upon final delivery

Agency will not begin work until the first payment is received.
```

**Late payments:**
```
Invoices unpaid after [15] days accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Agency 
reserves the right to pause work on overdue accounts until payment is received, 
without liability for resulting delays.
```

**Expenses:**
```
Client will reimburse pre-approved expenses within [30] days of receipt of 
expense report. Agency will obtain written approval for any single expense 
exceeding $[100] before incurring it.
```

---

### 3. Term and Termination

```
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term 
of [3 / 6 / 12] months ("Initial Term").

After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically on a month-to-month 
basis unless either party provides [30] days' written notice of non-renewal.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party 
materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within [14] 
days of written notice.

Client may terminate for convenience after the Initial Term with [30] days' 
written notice. Early termination during the Initial Term requires payment of 
[50%] of remaining retainer fees for the Initial Term.

Upon termination, Agency will deliver all completed work and Client materials 
within [10] business days.
```

---

### 4. Intellectual Property

```
Upon receipt of full payment, Agency assigns to Client all right, title, and 
interest in the deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement 
("Work Product"), including all intellectual property rights therein.

Agency retains ownership of:
  (a) Pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, processes, and methodologies 
      Agency uses to create the Work Product ("Agency Background IP")
  (b) General marketing knowledge and skills that are not specific to Client

Agency grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use any Agency 
Background IP embedded in the deliverables.

Client represents that any materials provided to Agency for use in the Services 
(brand assets, copy, data) do not infringe any third-party rights.
```

---

### 5. Revisions and Approvals

```
Each deliverable includes [2] rounds of revisions based on Client's written 
feedback. Additional revision rounds are billed at $[RATE]/hour.

A "revision" means changes within the original scope. Significant scope changes 
require a Change Order.

Client will provide feedback within [5] business days of receiving a draft. 
Failure to respond within this window is deemed approval.

Agency requires access to Client's platforms, accounts, and relevant data to 
perform the Services. Client agrees to provide this access within [3] business 
days of request.
```

---

### 6. Confidentiality

```
Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public information disclosed 
by the other party in connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information"), 
including business strategies, financials, customer data, and pricing.

Neither party will disclose Confidential Information to third parties without 
prior written consent, except as required by law or to the party's employees 
and contractors who need it to perform under this Agreement.

This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for [2] years.

Agency may identify Client as a client and describe the Services in general terms 
in its portfolio and marketing materials, unless Client requests otherwise in writing.
```

---

### 7. Non-Solicitation

```
During the term of this Agreement and for [12] months following its termination, 
Client agrees not to directly solicit or hire any Agency employee or contractor 
who worked on Client's account, without Agency's prior written consent.

Similarly, Agency agrees not to directly solicit Client's key employees during 
the same period.
```

---

### 8. Warranties and Representations

```
Each party represents that:
  (a) It has the legal authority to enter into this Agreement
  (b) This Agreement does not conflict with any other obligation

Agency represents that:
  (a) Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with 
      industry standards
  (b) Agency has the right to use all tools and materials used to perform 
      the Services

Client represents that:
  (a) All materials provided to Agency are owned by or licensed to Client
  (b) Client will provide timely access, feedback, and approvals as needed
```

---

### 9. Limitation of Liability

```
Neither party will be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, 
or punitive damages arising from this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility.

Agency's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by 
Client in the [3] months preceding the claim.

Agency does not guarantee specific results (rankings, conversion rates, revenue) 
from marketing Services. Marketing outcomes depend on factors outside Agency's 
control.
```

---

### 10. Dispute Resolution

```
The parties will attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation 
for [30] days before pursuing any other remedy.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State], without regard to conflict 
of law principles.

Any unresolved dispute will be settled by binding arbitration in [City, State] 
under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, except either party 
may seek injunctive relief in court.
```

---

### 11. General Provisions

```
Entire Agreement: This Agreement (including all Exhibits) constitutes the entire 
agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

Amendments: Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

Independent Contractor: Agency is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates 
an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship.

Severability: If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue 
in full force.

Notices: Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the signature block.
```

## Contract Checklist Before Sending

- [ ] Scope in Exhibit A matches the proposal exactly
- [ ] Out-of-scope items are explicit (ad spend, platform fees, extra revision rounds)
- [ ] Payment terms, amounts, and schedule are unambiguous
- [ ] Termination notice period and early termination fee are stated
- [ ] IP assignment is clear — who owns what after payment
- [ ] Attorney review recommended for engagements over $10k
