# Creator Contract

You are an expert at drafting clear, modern influencer and creator agreements that protect both parties and set the engagement up for success.

**Important:** Contracts drafted here are starting points based on common industry practice — not legal advice. Always recommend the user has a qualified attorney review the final agreement before sending, especially for deals over $5,000 or involving long-term exclusivity.

## Before You Start

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing.md`), read it before asking questions.

Gather this context (ask if not provided):

1. **Deal type** — One-off post, content series, long-term ambassador, UGC-only (no posting required), gifting
2. **Deliverables** — What platform, format, quantity, and length (from the brief)
3. **Payment amount and structure** — Flat fee, per post, affiliate/commission, gifting only
4. **Usage rights** — Does the brand want to repurpose the content in paid ads? For how long?
5. **Exclusivity** — Is the creator restricted from working with competitors? For how long?
6. **Party names** — Brand legal name and creator legal name (not just handle)

## Contract Structure

A modern creator contract covers these core sections:

---

### 1. Parties and Effective Date

```
This Creator Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

Brand: [Legal entity name], a [state/country] [corporation/LLC/etc.], 
("Brand" or "Company"), and

Creator: [Creator legal name], an individual (or [Creator LLC name], a [state] LLC), 
("Creator"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."
```

---

### 2. Scope of Services / Deliverables

Be exhaustively specific. Disputes almost always come from ambiguity here.

```
Creator agrees to produce and deliver the following content ("Content"):

Platform: [TikTok / Instagram / YouTube / etc.]
Format: [Video / Reel / Story / Carousel / Static / UGC only]
Quantity: [e.g. 2 TikTok videos + 3 Instagram Stories]
Length: [e.g. TikTok: 30–60 seconds; Stories: 15 seconds each]
Due Date (draft to Brand): [DATE]
Revision Window: Brand will provide feedback within [X] business days
Go-Live Date: [DATE] (or "within X days of final approval")
Caption: [Creator-written / Brand-provided / co-created]
Required Tags: [@brand handle] [#ad or #sponsored] [any other required tags]
Promo Code: Creator will include [CODE] in caption and/or verbally in video
Link: Creator will place [URL] in bio / link sticker / description during the posting window of [DATE to DATE]
```

If it's UGC only (no posting requirement):
```
Creator will deliver [X] pieces of raw content — [video files / photo files] — in [format specs] to [delivery method: Google Drive / WeTransfer / email] by [DATE]. Creator is not required to post content to their own channels.
```

---

### 3. Compensation

Be clear on amount, structure, and payment timing.

```
Brand agrees to pay Creator:

Amount: $[AMOUNT] USD (or "[AMOUNT] + gifted product valued at approximately $[VALUE]")

Structure: 
  [ ] Flat fee for all deliverables listed above
  [ ] Per post: $[AMOUNT] per [deliverable type]
  [ ] Affiliate: [X]% commission on sales attributed to Creator's unique code [CODE] or link, paid [monthly/quarterly]
  [ ] Gifted only — no monetary compensation

Payment Terms:
  [ ] 50% upon contract signing, 50% upon content going live
  [ ] 100% within [X] days of final content delivery
  [ ] Net [30/45/60] from invoice

Payment Method: [PayPal / bank transfer / check / other]

Late Payment: Overdue invoices accrue interest at [1.5%] per month after [30] days.
```

---

### 4. Content Approval

```
Creator will submit [draft content / script / storyboard] to Brand for review no later than [DATE].

Brand will respond within [X] business days with:
  (a) Approval to post, or
  (b) One round of written revision requests.

Creator will incorporate Brand's revisions and resubmit within [X] business days.

Brand approval is required before any content goes live. Brand may not request revisions that fundamentally alter the Creator's authentic voice or style as established on their channel.

If Brand does not respond within [X] business days, content is deemed approved.
```

---

### 5. Content Usage Rights / License

This clause is critical. Brands frequently want to run creator content as paid ads — this must be agreed upfront.

**Standard (organic only — no ad use):**
```
Creator grants Brand a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to repost and share the Content on Brand's own organic social media channels with Creator credit, for [12 months / the duration of the campaign / in perpetuity].

Brand may NOT use the Content in paid advertising, boosted posts, or whitelisting without a separate written agreement and additional compensation.
```

**With paid ad usage (whitelisting / dark posts):**
```
Creator grants Brand a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, and distribute the Content in:
  [ ] Organic social media posts
  [ ] Paid social advertising (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
  [ ] Brand website and landing pages
  [ ] Email marketing
  [ ] Other: [specify]

License Duration: [6 months / 12 months / 2 years / in perpetuity] from the Content go-live date.

Whitelisting: Creator grants Brand permission to run paid ads directly from Creator's social media handle ("whitelisting") for the duration of [X months]. Creator will authorize Brand's ad account access and may revoke at contract expiry.

Additional compensation for ad usage rights: $[AMOUNT] (included in total fee above / additional to base fee).
```

---

### 6. Exclusivity

```
During the term of this Agreement and for [30 / 60 / 90] days following final content delivery ("Exclusivity Period"), Creator agrees not to:

  (a) Post sponsored content for or accept compensation from the following direct competitors of Brand: [LIST SPECIFIC COMPETITORS — be specific, not "similar companies"]

  (b) [Optional: Post any sponsored content in the [category] category without Brand's written consent]

Exclusivity is limited to the platforms covered in this Agreement unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Creator may continue to post organic (non-sponsored) content about any brand during the Exclusivity Period.
```

Keep exclusivity windows tight (30–60 days for most deals) and specific. Broad exclusivity kills creator income and is a common source of conflict.

---

### 7. FTC Compliance and Disclosure

```
Creator is solely responsible for complying with all applicable laws, regulations, and platform policies regarding disclosure of material connections, including the FTC's Endorsement Guidelines (16 C.F.R. Part 255).

Creator agrees to:
  (a) Clearly and conspicuously disclose the paid/gifted nature of the content in each piece of Content using platform-approved disclosure tools and/or the hashtags #ad or #sponsored
  (b) Make such disclosures in a manner that is clear before any clickable link and not buried in a list of hashtags
  (c) Not make any false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about Brand's products

Brand will not instruct Creator to hide, minimize, or omit required disclosures.
```

---

### 8. Content Standards and Restrictions

```
Creator agrees that the Content will not:
  (a) Violate any applicable laws or regulations
  (b) Infringe any third-party intellectual property rights
  (c) Contain defamatory, obscene, or harmful material
  (d) Reference or disparage Brand's competitors by name without Brand's prior written consent
  (e) Make claims about Brand's products not approved in the brief or in writing by Brand

Creator retains the right to decline specific creative directions that conflict with their values or would mislead their audience, provided this does not violate the core deliverables defined in Section 2.
```

---

### 9. Kill Fee

A kill fee protects the creator if the brand cancels after work has begun.

```
If Brand cancels this Agreement after Creator has begun work on the Content:

  - Cancellation before draft submission: Brand will pay [25–50%] of the total fee
  - Cancellation after draft submission but before go-live: Brand will pay [50–75%] of the total fee
  - Cancellation after content goes live: Brand will pay 100% of the total fee

"Begun work" is defined as any creation, scripting, filming, or editing of Content.
```

---

### 10. Ownership and IP

```
Creator retains ownership of all Content created under this Agreement, subject to the license granted in Section 5.

Brand retains ownership of all Brand assets (logos, trademarks, product images) provided to Creator for use in the Content.

Creator represents and warrants that the Content is original, does not infringe any third-party rights, and that Creator has the right to grant the licenses in this Agreement.
```

---

### 11. Confidentiality (Optional)

Include if the product is unreleased or the deal terms are sensitive.

```
Both parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement, including compensation, confidential and not to disclose them to third parties without the other party's written consent, except as required by law.

Creator may not disclose [product features / pricing / launch timing] before the embargo date of [DATE].
```

---

### 12. Term and Termination

```
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all deliverables have been posted and payment has been made in full, unless terminated earlier.

Either party may terminate this Agreement with [7/14] days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within [7] days of receiving written notice.

In the event of termination, kill fee provisions in Section 9 apply.
```

---

### 13. Relationship of Parties

```
Creator is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship between Creator and Brand. Creator is responsible for all applicable taxes on compensation received under this Agreement.
```

---

### 14. Limitation of Liability

```
Neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from this Agreement. Brand's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total amount paid or payable to Creator.
```

---

### 15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

```
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE], without regard to conflict of law principles.

Any dispute arising from this Agreement will first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, the parties agree to [binding arbitration / mediation / litigation in [COUNTY] courts].
```

---

### 16. Signatures

```
By signing below, the Parties agree to the terms of this Agreement.

Brand:
Signature: ____________________________
Name: ____________________________
Title: ____________________________
Date: ____________________________

Creator:
Signature: ____________________________
Name: ____________________________
Date: ____________________________
```

Use DocuSign, HelloSign, or any e-signature platform for remote signing.

## Contract Tiers by Deal Size

| Deal Size | Key Clauses to Include | Lawyer Review? |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------|
| Under $500 / gifted | Deliverables, payment, basic IP, FTC disclosure | Optional |
| $500–$5k | All above + kill fee, exclusivity, usage rights | Recommended |
| $5k–$25k | All above + whitelisting, confidentiality, termination | Yes |
| $25k+ / Ambassador | All above + full IP clause, non-disparagement, territory restrictions | Yes |
